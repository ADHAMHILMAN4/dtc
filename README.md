# DTC — DreamTechCareer

DTC (DreamTechCareer) is a career management and IT job platform designed specifically for technology professionals.

The platform helps applicants discover IT jobs, manage resumes, track applications, prepare for interviews, and maintain their professional career profile.

Companies can manage job postings and applicants, while administrators manage the platform and external job synchronization.
